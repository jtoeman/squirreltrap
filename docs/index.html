The moment before the rabbit hole

Switching apps is exactly when focus slips. Squirrel Trap turns that split second into a quick check-in.

1

You press Cmd+Tab

Like you do a few hundred times a day, usually on autopilot.

2

A tiny box pops up

"What are you about to do?" Type it, dictate it, or just glance and keep going.

3

It becomes a checklist

Everything you name lands on a running list you can check off as you actually get to it.

Your to-dos stay on your Mac.

No account. No server. Your list is a plain file on your own disk, and nothing leaves your Mac unless you switch something on.

Squirrel Trap only listens for the Cmd+Tab keypress. It never records anything else you type.

  • Reminders sync: off by defaultShares your list with Apple's Reminders app, on your terms.
  • iCloud sync: off by defaultUses your private iCloud via Apple's CloudKit. No third parties.
  • Usage sharing: off by defaultAnonymous feature counts only. Never the text of your to-dos.

Questions

How do I install it?

Download the zip, unzip it, and drag Squirrel Trap.app wherever you like (Applications, Desktop, anywhere). It's signed and notarized, so it opens normally.

Why does it need Input Monitoring?

That's how macOS lets an app notice the Cmd+Tab keypress. It's listen-only, and Squirrel Trap ignores everything except that one shortcut. The app walks you through granting it on first launch. If it doesn't appear in System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, Input Monitoring, click +, and add the app manually.

Won't a pop-up every Cmd+Tab get annoying?

It's one small box you can dismiss instantly, and Snooze turns it off for as long as you want when you're heads-down. Most people find a gentle speed bump is exactly the point.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit from the menu bar icon and drag the app to the Trash. To clear your history too, delete ~/Library/Application Support/SquirrelTrap/ and ~/Library/Preferences/com.jtoeman.squirreltrap.plist.

Found a bug or have an idea?

This is an early, personal project and feedback is gold. Open a GitHub issue.
